About LucentStar AI

lucentstar.ai

LucentStar AI builds tools that help professionals communicate more clearly and consistently online.

Our first product, LucentSignal, helps professionals write LinkedIn posts that actually sound like them, not like everyone else on the platform. We built it because most AI writing tools optimise for volume and virality. We think the better goal is authenticity and clarity.

The idea behind LucentSignal

Most people who use LinkedIn professionally have something worth saying. They just don't have the time to turn a rough thought into something they'd actually want to publish. LucentSignal takes you from raw idea to a finished post, in a workflow that takes about four minutes.

Three writing styles. One post. You choose how it sounds.
