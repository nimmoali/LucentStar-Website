Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Applies to: website, blog and LucentSignal

This policy explains cookies and browser storage on lucentstar.ai, blog.lucentstar.ai and the LucentSignal sign-in, welcome and application pages. Read it alongside our Privacy Notice.

1. Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Local storage also lets a site remember a preference on your device.

2. Storage used by our services

3. Optional analytics

The website, blog, sign-in, welcome and signed-in app pages load Google Analytics only after you accept analytics. Rejecting analytics keeps it disabled on those pages. Withdrawing your choice disables further analytics and attempts to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies.

Your preference is stored separately on each service. Accepting analytics on the website does not accept it on the blog or app.

4. The signed-in application

LucentSignal uses a session cookie to maintain your signed-in state. Optional Google Analytics uses the same preference on sign-in, welcome and signed-in app pages. Reject prevents loading and optional app events. Withdrawal disables further analytics and removes accessible analytics cookies. Essential session cookies are retained.

5. Your choices

Use Cookie choices to accept or reject optional analytics. You can reopen these controls through Cookie settings in the footer. Closing the controls without accepting does not enable analytics. You can change your choice at any time.

You can also clear or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings. Blocking necessary authentication cookies can prevent you from signing in. Clearing preference storage means the service will ask for your choice again.

6. Updates and contact

We will update this policy as our storage and analytics arrangements change. Last updated: 3 October 2026.
